# Ridge Mill 24

The Ridge Mill 24 is a fictional manual coffee grinder.

- Direct price is $79 USD before tax and shipping.
- It has 24 stepped grind settings and uses a hand crank with no electricity.
- The grounds cup holds up to 25 g.
- Its settings work well for pour-over and French press. The steps are too coarse for reliably dialing in espresso with an unpressurized basket.
- Grinding takes manual effort. The manufacturer does not publish a grind-time estimate or waterproof rating.
- The limited manufacturer warranty lasts one year.
- Returns are accepted within 30 days for unused grinders purchased directly from the manufacturer.
